# Lex Arcanum

Homebrew wiki for **Baldur's Gate 3** and **D&D 5e (2024)**, collecting the material from the
*Lex Arcanum* supplement: original classes, subclasses, spells and items.

The first page published is the **Magister** — an Intelligence half-caster who channels spells
through an Arcane Gauntlet while fencing with a finesse blade.

**Live site: <https://fjorn17.github.io/Lex-Arcanum/>**

> ⚠️ **Everything here is a draft.** Features, numbers, spell lists and names are still being tested
> and rewritten, and may change or disappear between revisions. Nothing on this site is final.

## Contents

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | The Magister class page: overview, subclasses, progression table, Arcane Gauntlet, Schemas, spell list and level-by-level breakdown. |
| `css/style.css` | Every style on the site. Dark theme, Cinzel + Spectral typefaces. |
| `js/wiki-api.js` | Fills in the damage-type icons by querying the bg3.wiki API. Falls back to plain text offline. |
| `assets/img/` | Class crest and variants. |
| `pages/` | Reserved for the next pages of the supplement. |

## Viewing it

The site is published with GitHub Pages at <https://fjorn17.github.io/Lex-Arcanum/>.
You can also open `index.html` straight from a local copy in any browser. There is nothing to build and no dependencies to install.
Web fonts, bg3.wiki icons and the cover artwork are loaded from the internet — offline the page is
still perfectly readable, it just loses those images.

## Status

- **Magister (levels 1–12)** — written, being balanced.
- **Subclasses** — concept only. *Gematurge* is the one moving forward; *Weaver* and *Sigilist* are
  documented as ideas and will most likely be cut.
- **Rest of the supplement** — not started.

The complete, original material lives in the
[Lex Arcanum rulebook on Nivel20](https://nivel20.com/games/dnd-2024/rulebooks/2638-lex-arcanum)
(in Spanish).
