Word of Recall

Level 6 · Conjuration · SRD 5.2.1

Description

You and up to five willing creatures within 1.5 m (5 ft) of you instantly teleport to a previously designated sanctuary. You and any creatures that teleport with you appear in the nearest unoccupied space to the spot you designated when you prepared your sanctuary (see below). If you cast this spell without first preparing a sanctuary, the spell has no effect.

You must designate a location, such as a temple, as a sanctuary by casting this spell there.

How to learn

Classes whose spell list includes this spell:
