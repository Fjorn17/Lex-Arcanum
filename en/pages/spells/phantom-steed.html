Phantom Steed

Level 3 · Illusion · SRD 5.2.1

Description

A Large, quasi-real, horselike creature appears on the ground in an unoccupied space of your choice within range. You decide the creature’s appearance, and it is equipped with a saddle, bit, and bridle. Any of the equipment created by the spell vanishes in a puff of smoke if it is carried more than 3 m (10 ft) away from the steed.

For the duration, you or a creature you choose can ride the steed. The steed uses the Riding Horse stat block (see “Monsters”), except it has a Speed of 30 m (100 ft) and can travel 19.5 km (13 miles) in an hour. When the spell ends, the steed gradually fades, giving the rider 1 minute to dismount. The spell ends early if the steed takes any damage.

How to learn

Classes whose spell list includes this spell:

  • Wizard
  • Magister — supplement spell list.
