Wind Walk

Level 6 · Transmutation · SRD 5.2.1

Description

You and up to ten willing creatures of your choice within range assume gaseous forms for the duration, appearing as wisps of cloud. While in this cloud form, a target has a Fly Speed of 90 m (300 ft) and can hover; it has Immunity to the Prone condition; and it has Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage. The only actions a target can take in this form are the Dash action or a Magic action to begin reverting to its normal form. Reverting takes 1 minute, during which the target has the Stunned condition. Until the spell ends, the target can revert to cloud form, which also requires a Magic action followed by a 1-minute transformation.

If a target is in cloud form and flying when the effect ends, the target descends 18 m (60 ft) per round for 1 minute until it lands, which it does safely. If it can’t land after 1 minute, it falls the remaining distance.

How to learn

Classes whose spell list includes this spell:

  • Druid
