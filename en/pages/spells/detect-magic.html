Detect Magic

Level 1 · Divination · SRD 5.2.1

Description

For the duration, you sense the presence of magical effects within 9 m (30 ft) of yourself. If you sense such effects, you can take the Magic action to see a faint aura around any visible creature or object in the area that bears the magic, and if an effect was created by a spell, you learn the spell’s school of magic.

The spell is blocked by 1 foot of stone, dirt, or wood; 1 inch of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.

How to learn

Classes whose spell list includes this spell:

  • Bard
  • Cleric
  • Druid
  • Paladin
  • Ranger
  • Sorcerer
  • Warlock
  • Wizard
  • Magister — supplement spell list.
