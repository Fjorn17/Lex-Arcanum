Transmute Rock and Mud

Level 5 · Alchemy

Description

Choose an area of stone or mud that you can see within range and that fits in a 12 m (40 ft) Cube. You change it in one of the two following ways.

Mud to Stone. Nonmagical mud or quicksand in the area becomes as hard as ordinary stone. Each creature in the mud makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature is caught as the mud hardens and has the Restrained condition until it or another creature frees it with an action and a successful DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check.

Stone to Mud. Nonmagical stone in the area becomes an equal volume of thick mud. The area becomes Difficult Terrain, and a creature that enters it or ends its turn there sinks 1d1.2 m (4 ft), to a maximum of half its height. A creature that has sunk at least half its height has the Restrained condition and escapes as above.

Stone that is holding a structure up sags rather than gives way, and the GM decides what the structure does about it. When the spell ends, the material returns to what it was.
