Stinking Cloud

Level 3 · Uncatalogued

Uncatalogued

Poison probably belongs to Spiritism; it is not settled yet. It hangs from no Discipline. See uncatalogued.

Description

You create a 6 m (20 ft) radius Sphere of yellow, nauseating gas centered on a point within range. The cloud is Heavily Obscured. The cloud lingers in the air for the duration or until a strong wind (such as the one created by Gust of Wind) disperses it.

Each creature that starts its turn in the Sphere must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned condition until the end of the current turn. While Poisoned in this way, the creature can’t take an action or a Bonus Action.
