Spell Turning

Level 7 · Astronomy

Description

You keep a thin shell of gathered Ether around yourself and leave a hand ready to take hold of anyone else's. Astronomy reaches the Ether wherever the Ether is, including the Ether another hand is holding, and this spell does nothing more than that: it does not block a working, it takes it and hands it back.

For the duration, when a creature you can see casts a spell of level 6 or lower that targets only you, you can take a Reaction to seize it. That creature makes a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, the spell has no effect on you and instead takes effect as though its caster had cast it targeting themself, using their own spell save DC and attack bonus. On a successful save, the spell affects you as normal.

Once you have turned a spell of level 4 or higher in this way, this spell ends.

At higher levels

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You can turn a spell of level 7 or lower with a level 8 slot, or of level 8 or lower with a level 9 slot.
