Magic Armor

Level 3 · Astronomy

Description

You touch a suit of armor or a shield, imbuing it with a shimmering aura of divine protection.

You touch a nonmagical suit of armor or a shield. Until the spell ends, that item becomes a magic item with a +1 bonus to Armor Class.

The spell ends early if you lose your Concentration or if you have the Incapacitated condition. You can also dismiss this spell as a Bonus Action on your turn.

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th or 6th level, the bonus increases to +2. When you use a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the bonus increases to +3.
