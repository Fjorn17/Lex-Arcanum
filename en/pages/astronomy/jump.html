Jump

Level 1 · Cosmology

Description

You touch a willing creature. Once on each of its turns until the spell ends, that creature can jump up to 9 m (30 ft) by spending 3 m (10 ft) of movement.

At higher levels

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
