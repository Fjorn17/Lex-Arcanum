Gravity

Where what there is falls, and how hard

Two Branches, and the difference between them is who is being answered. Weight works on one body: how hard what is underneath pulls on it. Field works on a stretch of the world: which way everything inside it falls, or towards which single point.

The Well is the Principle at its own threshold — the Abyss made small and put somewhere on purpose. It is where this Discipline most obviously imitates what it is named after, and the one Star of the three Disciplines that is a base state pointed outwards.
