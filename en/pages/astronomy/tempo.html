Tempo

The passing of time over what is happening

Three Branches and one axis: what it reaches happens sooner than it would, later than it would, or stops happening for as long as the channelling lasts. Each of the first two has a Star for movement and a Star for the processes a body carries inside, and the second of each pair is the quieter and the more useful: blood that stops running from a wound, mortar that sets in an afternoon.

Suspension is the most expensive Star of the Discipline after the two of the Past, and against something alive it resolves like any other contest of Resilience. What is suspended does not move, fall, burn or bleed, and stays exactly where it was — a flame held halfway down is still burning, and catches whatever touches it the instant the channelling fails.
