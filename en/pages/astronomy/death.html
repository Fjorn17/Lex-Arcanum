Death

The soul gone, and the dying of tissue that is still part of someone

The Principle that is hunted almost everywhere, and the reason a necromancer rarely says out loud what they do. What it is hunted for is March: what is raised moves because the Ether moves it, and there is nobody inside. It does not remember, it does not obey beyond whatever is being imposed on it that instant, and it is not who it was.

Threshold is the whole of what this Discipline can do about death itself, and it is very little: it reaches a soul just gone, in the instant between the death and its scattering into the Ether. After that instant there is nothing to reach. No Star of this Principle, or of any other, brings anyone back — and every spell that claimed to has been struck from the supplement’s catalogue.

Necrosis is the exception that faces the other way: the only Star here that works on someone still alive, and the exact counterpart of everything the Principle of Life does. In many places the word necromancer and the word poison are used as if they meant the same.
