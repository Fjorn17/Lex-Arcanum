Mind

The mind of whatever feels

The widest Principle of the Discipline, and the one that holds the most of the supplement’s catalogue. Five Branches: what a body perceives and what can be put into or taken out of that perception; what it feels, read or imposed; what it keeps of what it lived; what it decides and what it goes on to do; and what it has left to hold anything up with.

Illusion and Veil are one Branch and one pair: what is not there put in, and what is there taken out. Everything the supplement files as illusion lives here, because an illusion is not a thing made in the world — it is a thing made in a mind.
