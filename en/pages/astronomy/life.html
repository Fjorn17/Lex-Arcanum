Life

The body that is still alive

What repairs in minutes what a body would repair by itself in weeks, and what drives out of a body whatever is consuming it. It is the shortest Principle of the Discipline and the least ambiguous: everything it does, a body could in principle have done alone, given time it does not have.

Its ceiling is the death rule. Once the soul has scattered there is nothing left to work on, so this Principle stops exactly where the Principle of Death begins — and neither of the two crosses back.
