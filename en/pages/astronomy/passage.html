Passage

Two points of the world set side by side

The Principle of distance annulled. One Star, one Branch, and the largest share of the supplement’s catalogue of any subdivision of this Discipline.

Seeing at a distance counts here. A remote sensor is two points of the world set side by side, which is exactly what this Principle does; it simply sends sight across instead of a body. Finding something far off is the same operation with nothing sent at all.

Its second Branch, Pocket, has been struck out: it made a closed space that is nowhere, and there is no space apart from this one. What is left is a way from here to there, open only for as long as somebody keeps feeding it.
