Faithful Hound

Level 4 · Uncatalogued

Uncatalogued

Summoning, and left behind unfed besides. It hangs from no Discipline. See uncatalogued.

Description

You conjure a phantom watchdog in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. The hound remains for the duration or until the two of you are more than 90 m (300 ft) apart from each other.

No one but you can see the hound, and it is intangible and invulnerable. When a Small or larger creature comes within 9 m (30 ft) of it without first speaking the password that you specify when you cast this spell, the hound starts barking loudly. The hound has Truesight with a range of 9 m (30 ft).

At the start of each of your turns, the hound attempts to bite one enemy within 1.5 m (5 ft) of it. That enemy must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 4d8 Force damage.

On your later turns, you can take a Magic action to move the hound up to 9 m (30 ft).
