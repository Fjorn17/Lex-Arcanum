Threadmarshal

Run the cord first. The battle goes where the line lets it go.

In development

Rebuilt from the original Urdidor. Numbers and wording are drafts.

Armies have always had someone whose job is the ground rather than the enemy: the officer who decides where the ditch goes, which bridge stays up, and which lane the cavalry will never get to use. In the order that officer carries cord.

A Threadmarshal walks a field before anyone draws, and leaves it strung. Line to a rafter, line to a gatepost, line into the sword arm of whoever is going to matter. Nothing about it is subtle once it starts: the cord goes taut, a charge folds in on itself, and a spell that should have needed a hand laid on a throat arrives down thirty feet of wire instead. The order's other disciplines beat the enemy. This one decides where the enemy is allowed to stand.

Level 3 Threadmarshal Spells

You gain additional spells when you reach certain levels in this class. These count as Magister spells for you, do not count against the number of spells you can prepare, and cannot be replaced when you gain a level.

Level 1

  • Ensnaring Strike
  • Compelled Duel

Level 2

  • Web
  • Hold Person

Level 3

  • Chains of Custody
  • Slow

Level 4

  • Magic Contract
  • Resilient Sphere

Level 5

  • Telekinesis
  • Hold Monster

Ensnaring Strike, Web, Hold Person and Hold Monster are not otherwise on the Magister spell list. The rest already are, and the subclass simply keeps them prepared for you.

Level 3 Field Rigging

Cord, and the two trades that make it worth carrying.

Tool Proficiency. You gain proficiency with weaver's tools and carpenter's tools. If you already have either proficiency, you gain proficiency with another type of artisan's tools of your choice.

Quick Work. The time you need to craft an item with weaver's tools or carpenter's tools is halved.

Marionette Proficiency. You gain proficiency with marionettes.

Strings. As a Bonus Action, you can run a string onto a creature you can see within 9 m (30 ft) of yourself. That creature is Strung by you for 1 minute.

You can also run a string with no action required whenever a creature fails a saving throw against a Magister spell you cast.

You can have a number of creatures Strung equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of one). If you run a string beyond that number, your oldest string ends.

Ending a String. A string ends early if the creature is more than 9 m (30 ft) from you at the end of your turn, if you end it (no action required), or if you have the Incapacitated condition.

Design note: one moving part

There is nothing to spend and nothing to track but the strings themselves — how many creatures are on the line, and since when. Every other feature of this subclass reads off that one state.

Level 3 Against the Grain

A rope is not an argument. It does not care whether you find it persuasive.

Down the Line. When you cast a Magister spell that has a range of Touch, you can target a creature Strung by you instead of a creature you touch.

A Physical Problem. When a creature Strung by you makes a saving throw against a Magister spell you cast, and that spell would Restrain it, Incapacitate it, Paralyze it, Charm it, Frighten it, move it, or reduce its Speed, it makes a Strength saving throw in place of an Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma saving throw.

Such a spell can affect the creature even if it is immune to the Charmed or Frightened condition.

Level 7 Wire's Edge

The cord carries the edge to where the arm does not reach.

Reach of the Line. Your reach with Finesse melee weapons is 9 m (30 ft) against creatures Strung by you.

Still a Melee Attack. An attack you make at that reach counts as a melee weapon attack for every purpose.

Running Rigging. When you take the Attack action, you can run a string as part of one of those attacks instead of using a Bonus Action.

Design note: what that unlocks

Because those attacks are melee weapon attacks, they carry every rider the class already has — Arcane Swordplay, En Garde, Touché and your Weapon Mastery properties all work from 9 m as if you were standing in front of the target. That is the whole point of the feature, and it adds no damage of its own.

Level 15 Taut

You stopped needing a spell to hold the state. The lines hold it for you.

Strung Trance. While at least one creature is Strung by you, your Instinctive Trance is active even if you are not concentrating on a spell.

Check the Line. When a creature Strung by you moves, you can use your Reaction to haul on the cord. The creature makes a Strength saving throw against your spell save DC.

Failure: Its Speed becomes 0 until the end of its turn, and you pull it up to 3 m (10 ft) in a straight line toward yourself.

Success: Its Speed is halved until the end of its turn.

Level 20 Marshalled Field

You do not fight on the ground any more. You issue it.

No Slack. There is no limit to the number of creatures you can have Strung, and your strings last until you end them or you have the Incapacitated condition.

Lattice. As a Magic action, you lace the ground and the air within 18 m (60 ft) of yourself with cord. Each creature of your choice that you can see in that area becomes Strung by you and makes a Strength saving throw against your spell save DC.

Failure: The creature has the Restrained condition until the end of your next turn.

Success: The creature's Speed is halved until the end of your next turn.

The lattice lasts 1 minute. While it lasts, a creature Strung by you that moves more than 3 m (10 ft) on its turn takes 4d8 Slashing damage.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

What became of the Urdidor

The two mechanics worth keeping were the ones the original built its identity on: strings that mark a target, and control spells that resolve as a physical problem instead of a mental one. Both are here. The blood, the animated dead, the charge pool and the puppet companion are not.

Detrás de los hilos Kept nearly intact as Against the Grain, including the clause that lets a binding land on a creature immune to being Charmed. This is the centre of the subclass now, not a rider.

Why levels 7, 15 and 20 grant no spells

Only level 3 touches the spell list. Everything after it either extends a feature the Magister already has — Wire's Edge hands Arcane Swordplay, En Garde and Touché a 9 m reach, Strung Trance unhooks Instinctive Trance from concentration — or does something the base class cannot do at all, which is stop a creature where it stands without spending anything.

Notes for the balance pass

This subclass has not been measured. Two things to watch.

Wire's Edge is the big one. Melee weapon attacks at 9 m, carrying every rider the class has, is a large change to how a Magister stands in a fight — and it does it without adding a single point of damage, which is deliberate: the current analysis already has the class ahead on sustained damage. If it proves too strong, the lever is the range, not the feature.

Against the Grain cuts both ways. Swapping a mental save for Strength is a gift against high-Wisdom casters and a liability against brutes. That trade is the point, and it should stay a trade — if it ever reads as pure upside, the immunity clause is what to look at first.
