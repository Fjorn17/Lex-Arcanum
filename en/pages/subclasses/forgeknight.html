Forgeknight

Forge magic into steel, and become the wall that does not break.

Runefire is the order's first secret: a cold blue flame that scars sigils into metal instead of melting it, binding the steel to the mind that shaped it. Forgeknights spend their years at the anvil turning that bond into armor, blades, and reliquaries built to survive what breaks lesser things — then carry all three wherever the line needs holding.

Level 3 Forgeknight Spells

You gain additional spells when you reach certain levels in this class. These count as Magister spells for you, do not count against the number of spells you can prepare, and cannot be replaced when you gain a level.

Level 1

  • Identify
  • Bound Weapon

Level 2

  • Magic Weapon
  • Spiritual Weapon

Level 3

  • Elemental Weapon
  • Magic Armor

Level 4

  • Secret Chest
  • Faithful Hound

Level 5

  • Steel Wind Strike
  • Teleportation Circle

Level 3 Runeforged Craft

You've learned to work metal the way your order works magic: with fire, patience, and precision.

Tool Proficiency. You gain proficiency with smith's tools and jeweler's tools. If you already have either of these proficiencies, you gain proficiency with another type of artisan's tools of your choice.

Heavy Metal. You gain proficiency with heavy armor, shields, and all martial melee weapons.

Level 3 Ironclad Vanguard

Weight becomes a weapon in your hands, and your armor answers as readily as your blade.

Rune Shield. While you have the benefit of your Shield spell, you count as wielding a shield.

Shield from the Line. When you would use a Reaction that requires you to be wielding a shield, you can cast Shield as part of that Reaction.

Brutish Fencing. You can use your Arcane Swordplay feature with any melee weapon you are proficient with. The weapon does not need the Finesse property.

Level 7 Runefire Temper

The fire in your armor has learned your will, and answers to it even under fire.

Flare. When you cast a spell of level 1 or higher, you can use your Reaction to gain Temporary Hit Points equal to the level of the spell slot plus your Intelligence modifier.

Rooted Stance. While you are wearing Heavy armor and concentrating on a spell, you can't be given the Prone condition, and any forced movement you are subjected to is halved.

Level 15 Lodestone Ember

A single word of command turns your runefire inward, unbinding your armor's weight without dulling its edge.

As a Bonus Action, your armor drops one category — Heavy becomes Medium, Medium becomes Light — gaining the following benefits for 10 minutes. Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you expend a spell slot of level 3 or higher to restore its use (no action required).

Tempered Weight. Your armor's weight, Strength requirement, and Stealth property are treated as those of its new, lighter category.

Freed Stride. The maximum Dexterity modifier you can add to your Armor Class increases to match its new, lighter category.

Unshackled Instinct. Your armor also counts as its new, lighter category for any feature keyed to your armor's weight, such as Instinctive Trance.

Level 20 Walking Fortress

Shield Wall. When you make a Dexterity, Constitution, or Strength saving throw against a spell that deals half damage on a success, you can use your Reaction to add your shield's Armor Class bonus to that saving throw. You must be holding a shield.

Failure: You take half damage.

Success: You take no damage.
