Crystal

Adventuring Gear (arcane focus)

Description

A crystal focused and calibrated for channeling raw magical energy, serving as a conduit for spellcasting.

An arcane crystal can be used as a spellcasting focus by a spellcaster. A typical crystal is composed of naturally formed quartz or a synthetically grown mineral lattice infused with stable resonance nodes, allowing the user to bypass material components that lack a gold cost.

Properties

  • Type: Arcane focus
  • Rarity: Common
  • Attunement: No
  • Weight: 0.5 kg (1 lb)
  • Price: 10 gp
