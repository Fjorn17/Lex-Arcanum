# Wall of Light

*Muro de luz*

| | |
| --- | --- |

| **Llena un hueco** | no |

## Qué hacía

Un muro de luz brillante de 60 pies que ciega, daña con radiante y puede desmontarse en un rayo.

## Por qué encaja aquí

Luz densa sostenida en pie. El sistema dice que el Éter alumbra gratis y pesa carísimo (Reglas 1.4.2): un muro que ciega pero no detiene es exactamente lo que sale de ahí, y explica por qué la astronomía no puede hacer un muro de verdad.

## Versión 5.5e

Lista para pegar en `doc/spells/srd-en.txt`, con `== Wall of Light` de cabecera.

```
level: 5
school: Evocation
casting: Action
range: 120 feet
components: V, S, M (a shard of clear crystal)
duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
body:
A wall of gathered light springs into being: up to 60 feet long, 10 feet high and 5 feet thick, or a ringed wall up to 20 feet in diameter. It sheds Bright Light in a 120-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 120 feet.
When the wall appears, each creature in its area makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 4d8 Radiant damage on a failed save or half as much on a success. A creature that fails also has the Blinded condition until the spell ends; it can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the condition on itself on a success.
A creature that enters the wall or ends its turn there makes the same save.
As an Action, you can dismiss the wall and shape what is left into a beam 60 feet long and 5 feet wide. Each creature in the beam makes the save as above; the spell then ends.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 5.
```
