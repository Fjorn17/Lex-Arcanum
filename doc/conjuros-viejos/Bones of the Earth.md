# Bones of the Earth

*Huesos de la tierra*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **De dónde sale** | 5e (Xanathar) |
| **Dónde iría** | Alquimia › Tierra |
| **Llena un hueco** | no |
| **Cruza un segundo umbral** | no |

## Qué hacía

Hasta seis columnas de piedra brotan del suelo, levantan a quien esté encima y aplastan a quien quede atrapado contra un techo.

## Por qué encaja aquí

Roca a escala de terreno. Y es de los pocos conjuros de cualquier edición donde lo que se fabrica se queda en el mundo y se puede usar después, que es una tensión sana con la regla de que nada dura solo.

## Versión 5.5e

Lista para pegar en `doc/spells/srd-en.txt`, con `== Bones of the Earth` de cabecera.

```
level: 6
school: Transmutation
casting: Action
range: 120 feet
components: V, S
duration: Instantaneous
body:
You raise up to six pillars of stone from ground you can see within range. Each is a Cylinder 5 feet in diameter and up to 30 feet high, and each must have its base on ground or on another pillar raised by this spell.
If a pillar rises under a creature, that creature can make a Dexterity saving throw to move off it; otherwise it rides the pillar up.
If a creature would be pressed against a ceiling, it makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 6d6 Bludgeoning damage on a failed save and being Restrained between the pillar and the ceiling; on a success it moves to the nearest unoccupied space and takes half as much damage.
A pillar has AC 5 and 30 hit points. When reduced to 0, it crumbles into rubble that makes its area Difficult Terrain.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You raise two additional pillars for each spell slot level above 6.
```
