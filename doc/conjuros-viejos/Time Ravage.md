## Qué hacía

Envejece brutalmente a una criatura: daño necrótico y, si falla, queda a un paso de la muerte por vejez hasta que la curen.

## Por qué encaja aquí

Llena el Principio de Edad, hoy vacío y documentado como vacío. Es Senescencia sin adornos: los años que se le imponen a un cuerpo.

## Versión 5.5e

Lista para pegar en `doc/spells/srd-en.txt`, con `== Time Ravage` de cabecera.

```
level: 9
school: Necromancy
casting: Action
range: 90 feet
components: V, S, M (an hourglass of black sand)
duration: Instantaneous
body:
You pile years onto one creature you can see within range. The target makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 10d12 Necrotic damage on a successful save.
On a failed save, the target takes the damage and ages to the brink: its hair whitens, its skin draws tight and its joints stiffen. It has Disadvantage on ability checks and saving throws, its Speed is halved, and unless the ageing is undone it dies in a number of days equal to its Constitution modifier (minimum 1 day).
A Greater Restoration or Wish spell ends the ageing but not the damage.
```
