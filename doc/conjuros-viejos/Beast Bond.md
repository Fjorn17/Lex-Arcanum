# Beast Bond

## Qué hacía

Vínculo telepático con una bestia de Inteligencia 3 o menor: se entienden y ambos atacan mejor a los mismos enemigos.

## Por qué encaja aquí

Es el Pacto del sistema, casi palabra por palabra: se sella con un animal que lo admita, no se impone, y lo que pasa por él es intención, no orden.

## Versión 5.5e

Lista para pegar en `doc/spells/srd-en.txt`, con `== Beast Bond` de cabecera.

```
level: 1
school: Divination
casting: Action
range: Touch
components: V, S, M (a bit of fur wrapped in cloth)
duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
body:
You touch a Beast that has Intelligence 3 or lower. For the duration, the two of you share a bond: it understands what you want of it, though not your words, and you know what it perceives.
While you and the Beast are within 60 feet of one another, each of you has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature the other of you attacked since the end of your last turn.
The bond is not a leash. The Beast acts as its nature allows, and a Beast that would not have you cannot be a target.
```
