# Enemies Abound

*Enemigos por todas partes*

| | |

## Qué hacía

La criatura pierde la capacidad de distinguir amigo de enemigo y ataca a lo que tenga más cerca.

## Por qué encaja aquí

Sujeción sin decirle a nadie qué hacer: no impone un acto, quita una distinción. Es más interesante que dominar, y más barato de justificar en un sistema donde controlar a alguien es carísimo.

## Versión 5.5e

Lista para pegar en `doc/spells/srd-en.txt`, con `== Enemies Abound` de cabecera.

```
level: 3
school: Enchantment
casting: Action
range: 120 feet
components: V, S
duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
body:
You reach into the mind of one creature you can see within range and take away its sense of who is who. The target makes an Intelligence saving throw. On a successful save, nothing happens.
On a failed save, the target has the Frightened condition for the duration, and it treats every creature it can see as an enemy. On each of its turns it must use its action to attack the creature nearest to it, choosing at random between equally near targets. At the end of each of its turns, it repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success.
```
