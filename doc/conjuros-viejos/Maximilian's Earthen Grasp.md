# Maximilian’s Earthen Grasp

*Garra de tierra de Maximilian*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **De dónde sale** | 5e (Xanathar) |
| **Dónde iría** | Alquimia › Tierra |
| **Llena un hueco** | no |
| **Cruza un segundo umbral** | no |

## Qué hacía

Una mano gigante de tierra sale del suelo, agarra a una criatura, la apresa y la aplasta cada turno.

## Por qué encaja aquí

Roca con Cohesión, que es la pareja de Estrellas que define el elemento Tierra. Y llena el hueco de un conjuro de control de nivel bajo que no sea de plantas ni de hielo.

## Versión 5.5e

Lista para pegar en `doc/spells/srd-en.txt`, con `== Maximilian’s Earthen Grasp` de cabecera.

```
level: 2
school: Transmutation
casting: Action
range: 30 feet
components: V, S, M (a handful of dirt)
duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
body:
A Medium hand of packed earth rises from the ground at a point you can see within range and grasps at one creature you can see within 5 feet of it. The target makes a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 2d6 Bludgeoning damage and has the Restrained condition for the duration.
As an Action on your later turns, you can crush the restrained target, dealing 2d6 Bludgeoning damage, or move it up to 30 feet in any direction within the spell's range.
A restrained creature can use an Action to make a Strength (Athletics) check against your spell save DC, escaping on a success.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.
```
