# Mold Earth

*Moldear tierra*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **De dónde sale** | 5e (Xanathar) |
| **Dónde iría** | Alquimia › Tierra |
| **Llena un hueco** | no |
| **Cruza un segundo umbral** | no |

## Qué hacía

Truco elemental: mueve un cubo de tierra suelta, la endurece o la ablanda, o graba formas en ella. Sin daño.

## Por qué encaja aquí

El sistema necesita trucos que no hagan daño para que Asterismo signifique algo (Reglas 7.0.6): magia cotidiana que sale del flujo pasivo. Los cuatro trucos elementales de Xanathar son el mejor material que hay para eso.

## Versión 5.5e

Lista para pegar en `doc/spells/srd-en.txt`, con `== Mold Earth` de cabecera.

```
level: 0
school: Transmutation
casting: Action
range: 30 feet
components: S
duration: Instantaneous or 1 hour
body:
Choose a portion of dirt or stone you can see within range that fits within a 5-foot Cube. You work it in one of the following ways:
• If you target loose earth, you move it up to 10 feet. This does not deal damage.
• You cause shapes, colors or both to appear on the earth or stone, spelling out words, creating an image or shaping a pattern. The change lasts for 1 hour.
• If the earth or stone you target is on the ground, you make it Difficult Terrain, or you reverse Difficult Terrain you created this way. The change lasts for 1 hour.
You can have no more than two of this spell's non-instantaneous effects active at a time, and you can dismiss one as an Action.
```
