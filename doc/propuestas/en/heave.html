Heave

Level 4 · Astronomy

Description

A state of force put on the Ether does nothing at all until it is handed over. You put one on the air around a loose object and then let go.

Choose up to three objects you can see within range that are not being worn or carried and that each weigh 50 pounds or less. Each one is hurled at a creature or point you choose within 18 m (60 ft) of it. For each object thrown at a creature, make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 3d8 Bludgeoning damage, or 3d8 Piercing or Slashing damage if the object is sharp.

Objects thrown by this spell land where they end up. Nothing is held, carried, or moved gently: this is a push and a release, and the spell has no way to set anything down.

At higher levels

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You can throw one additional object for each spell slot level above 4.
