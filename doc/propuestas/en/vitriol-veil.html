Vitriol Veil

Level 4 · Alchemy

Description

Mist and acid meet at the Crucible, and what comes out hangs in the air around you.

For the duration, a 4.5 m (15 ft) Emanation of pale, sour mist moves with you. The area is Lightly Obscured to anyone but you.

When a creature enters the Emanation for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there, it makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 3d6 Acid damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

A creature that hits you with a melee attack while you are in the mist takes 2d6 Acid damage.

At higher levels

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The damage of both effects increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 4.
