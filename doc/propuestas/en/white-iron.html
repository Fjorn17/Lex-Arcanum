White Iron

Level 1 · Alchemy

Description

Fire and discharge have this in common: they shine while they are consumed. Here the shining is left in the metal, which goes on being metal the whole time.

You touch a weapon, and for the duration it glows white-hot. It sheds Bright Light in a 6 m (20 ft) radius and Dim Light for another 6 m (20 ft), and its attacks deal an extra 1d6 Fire damage on a hit.

The weapon cannot be sheathed, put in a pack or set down on anything that burns, and its wielder knows exactly where in the dark everyone else is looking.

At higher levels

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The extra damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
