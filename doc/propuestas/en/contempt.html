Contempt

Level 4 · Spiritism

Description

Mood does not dictate acts, and this one dictates nothing at all. It simply makes everyone else not worth the trouble.

One creature you can see within range makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, for the duration it holds every other creature in open contempt: it has Disadvantage on attack rolls against any creature that has not damaged it since its last turn, it cannot be helped — it refuses the Help action and any healing offered — and it has Disadvantage on ability checks made as part of a group effort.

The creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

At higher levels

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You can affect one additional creature for each spell slot level above 4.
