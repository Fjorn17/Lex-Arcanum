Charge on the Skin

Level 4 · Alchemy

Description

You hold a thin charge across your own skin for the duration. It is the cheapest thing lightning does and the only one that can be kept up for minutes at a time, because almost nothing is being asked of it.

When a creature within 1.5 m (5 ft) of you hits you with a melee attack, it takes 3d8 Lightning damage.

As an action, you can end the spell early and discharge it all at once: each creature within 4.5 m (15 ft) of you makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 6d8 Lightning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

At higher levels

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. Both damage values increase by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 4.
