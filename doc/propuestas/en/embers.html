Embers

Level 5 · Alchemy

Description

Molten rock thrown high enough comes down as rain, and this is the smallest amount of it worth the crossing.

A 6 m (20 ft) radius, 12 m (40 ft)-high Cylinder centered on a point you choose within range fills with falling cinders for the duration. The area is Lightly Obscured and is Difficult Terrain.

When a creature enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d6 Fire damage and 2d6 Bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much of both on a successful one.

Anything flammable in the area that is not being worn or carried catches fire.

At higher levels

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The Fire damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 5.
