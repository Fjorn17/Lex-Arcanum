Ash Storm

Level 3 · Alchemy

Description

You press gathered Ether until it crosses into ash and let it fall. A 6 m (20 ft) radius, 6 m (20 ft)-high Cylinder centered on a point you choose within range fills with falling ash for the duration.

The area is Lightly Obscured, and it is Difficult Terrain. A creature that starts its turn in the area makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 2d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one, as the hot ash works its way in.

A creature in the area has Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight, and any open flame in the area is smothered.

At higher levels

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3.
