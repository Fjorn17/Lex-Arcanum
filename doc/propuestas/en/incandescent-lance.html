Incandescent Lance

Level 3 · Alchemy

Description

Matter shining from its own heat, drawn out into a thread and sent. It is not light, and being in shadow is no help at all.

Make a ranged spell attack against one creature or object you can see within range. On a hit, the target takes 7d6 Fire damage.

On a hit against a creature, the target also glows: until the end of your next turn it sheds Dim Light in a 3 m (10 ft) radius, it cannot benefit from the Invisible condition or from Heavily Obscured terrain, and attack rolls against it have Advantage.

At higher levels

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3.
