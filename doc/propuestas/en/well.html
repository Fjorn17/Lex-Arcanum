Well

Level 5 · Cosmology

Description

The Well is the Principle standing on its own threshold: the Abyss made small and put somewhere on purpose. It is the one place in the whole art where a base state is turned outwards and used as an effect.

Choose a point you can see within range. For the duration, everything within 9 m (30 ft) of it falls towards it instead of downwards.

When the Well appears, and when a creature enters that radius or starts its turn there, it makes a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, it is pulled 6 m (20 ft) towards the point and takes 3d10 Force damage. On a successful save, it is pulled 3 m (10 ft) and takes half as much damage.

A creature that starts its turn within 1.5 m (5 ft) of the point has the Restrained condition and takes 3d10 Force damage, and loose objects in the radius are dragged in and pile up there.

At higher levels

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 5.
