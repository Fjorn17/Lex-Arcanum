== Ether Theft
level: 4
school: Abjuration
classes: Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard
casting: Action
range: 60 feet
components: V, S
duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
body:
Astronomy reaches the Ether wherever the Ether is, and that includes the Ether another hand is holding. You close your fingers on somebody else's work and pull.
Choose one creature you can see that is maintaining Concentration on a spell of level 3 or lower. It makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, its Concentration ends and you take the spell up yourself: it continues under your Concentration, with you choosing its targets where it allows a choice, for the rest of its original duration or the duration of this spell, whichever ends first.
If the stolen spell is one you could not otherwise cast, you still hold it, but you cannot change anything it is already doing.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You can take a spell of one level higher for each spell slot level above 4.

== Dimming

school: Abjuration

casting: Action
range: 60 feet
components: V, S, M (a wick pinched out between two fingers)
duration: 1 hour
body:

A Dimmed creature knows what has been done to it from the first time it tries to cast. It can take an action on each of its turns to repeat the Charisma save, ending the effect on itself on a success.

casting: Action
range: 60 feet
components: V, S
duration: Instantaneous
body:

== Interrogation
level: 2
school: Divination
classes: Bard, Cleric, Wizard
casting: 1 minute
range: Touch
components: V, S
duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
body:
You put your hand on a creature that has the Incapacitated condition, or a willing one, and ask.
For the duration, you can ask the target up to five questions, one per minute. For each question, the target makes a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, the memory the question reaches comes up whether the target wants it to or not, and you see it as the target saw it.

components: V, S, M (a nettle leaf)
duration: Instantaneous
body:
Sprouting governs how a bonded plant grows, and nothing says the growing has to be slow.
Nettles burst out of the ground around you. Each creature other than you in a 10-foot Emanation makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 3d6 Piercing damage and 1d6 Poison damage, and its Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, it takes half as much of both and its Speed is not reduced.
The ground in the Emanation becomes Difficult Terrain for 1 minute, and then the nettles wither.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The Piercing damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.

== Delay Pain
level: 2
school: Abjuration
classes: Bard, Cleric, Druid
casting: Action
range: Touch
components: V, S
duration: 1 hour
body:
Nothing is healed. The wound is exactly as bad as it was; the body has simply been talked out of reporting it for a while.
For the duration, the willing creature you touch ignores the effects of pain: it does not have Disadvantage from an injury, it does not lose Concentration from taking damage unless it drops to 0 Hit Points, and it can act normally while at 1 Hit Point.
When the spell ends, everything arrives at once. The creature has the Exhausted condition with 1 Exhaustion level, and 2 instead if it took more than half its Hit Point maximum in damage during the duration.

== Diagnose Illness
level: 1
school: Divination
classes: Cleric, Druid, Ranger
casting: Action
range: Touch
components: S
duration: Instantaneous
body:
You read the pulse of a creature you touch and are told what is wrong with it: every disease, poison and curse it is carrying, how long it has had each one, and how long it has left if nothing is done.
You are told nothing about how to fix any of it.
The reading is of the body and not of the mind: it finds a fever and a venom and misses a grief entirely.

== Howling Agony
level: 2
school: Necromancy
classes: Bard, Cleric, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
casting: Action
range: 60 feet
components: V, S
duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
body:
One creature you can see within range makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, for the duration it is in pain that has no cause and no end.
At the start of each of its turns, the creature takes 2d8 Psychic damage, and on that turn it can take either an action or a Bonus Action, not both.
The creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. A creature that cannot feel pain is unaffected.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 2.
