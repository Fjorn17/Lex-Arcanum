== Ether Theft
level: 4
school: Abjuration
classes: Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard
casting: Action
range: 60 feet
components: V, S
duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
body:
Astronomy reaches the Ether wherever the Ether is, and that includes the Ether another hand is holding. You close your fingers on somebody else's work and pull.
Choose one creature you can see that is maintaining Concentration on a spell of level 3 or lower. It makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, its Concentration ends and you take the spell up yourself: it continues under your Concentration, with you choosing its targets where it allows a choice, for the rest of its original duration or the duration of this spell, whichever ends first.
If the stolen spell is one you could not otherwise cast, you still hold it, but you cannot change anything it is already doing.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You can take a spell of one level higher for each spell slot level above 4.

== Dimming
level: 5
school: Abjuration
classes: Bard, Cleric, Wizard
casting: Action
range: 60 feet
components: V, S, M (a wick pinched out between two fingers)
duration: 1 hour
body:
You thin the Ether inside a body until its own network has to work in a halo of its making. One creature you can see makes a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, it is Dimmed for the duration.
While Dimmed, a creature that casts a spell must first make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, the spell fails, and the slot is spent.
A Dimmed creature knows what has been done to it from the first time it tries to cast. It can take an action on each of its turns to repeat the Charisma save, ending the effect on itself on a success.

== Etheric Blow
level: 3
school: Evocation
classes: Sorcerer, Wizard
casting: Action
range: Self (5-foot cone)
components: V, S
duration: Instantaneous
body:
You press a gathered Star until it weighs, and hand it over into the space in front of you. Each creature in a 5-foot Cone makes a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 5d6 Force damage and is pushed up to 20 feet away from you and knocked Prone. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only and is not pushed.
A creature pushed into a solid obstacle takes an extra 1d6 Force damage and stops there.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3.

== Heave
level: 4
school: Evocation
classes: Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard
casting: Action
range: 60 feet
components: V, S
duration: Instantaneous
body:
A state of force put on the Ether does nothing at all until it is handed over. You put one on the air around a loose object and then let go.
Choose up to three objects you can see within range that are not being worn or carried and that each weigh 50 pounds or less. Each one is hurled at a creature or point you choose within 60 feet of it. For each object thrown at a creature, make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 3d8 Bludgeoning damage, or 3d8 Piercing or Slashing damage if the object is sharp.
Objects thrown by this spell land where they end up. Nothing is held, carried, or moved gently: this is a push and a release, and the spell has no way to set anything down.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You can throw one additional object for each spell slot level above 4.

== Acid Dew
level: 4
school: Transmutation
classes: Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard

components: V, S, M (a drop of oil of vitriol)
duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
body:
Not a mist, whatever it looks like from across a room. Acid is its own crossing, of Water and Lightning, and what hangs in the air here is acid in droplets too small to fall.
For the duration, a 15-foot Emanation moves with you. When a creature enters it for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there, it makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 3d6 Acid damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

== Ice Snare
level: 5
school: Transmutation
classes: Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard
casting: Action
range: 120 feet
components: V, S, M (a chip of clear ice)
duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
body:
The damp in the air around one creature is gathered in and set, all at once, so that what holds it is made out of the room it was standing in.
The target makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 4d6 Cold damage and has the Restrained condition for the duration. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only.
A creature Restrained this way takes 2d6 Cold damage at the start of each of its turns. It can take an action to make a Strength (Athletics) check against your spell save DC, breaking out on a success.
The ice is an object with AC 15 and 30 Hit Points and it has Vulnerability to Fire damage. That vulnerability is not a weakness anyone chose: it is the countdown every Mist-worker is taught first, and a torch held against the snare undoes in seconds what the spell spent a level on.

== Interrogation
level: 2
school: Divination
classes: Bard, Cleric, Wizard
casting: 1 minute
range: Touch
components: V, S
duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
body:
You put your hand on a creature that has the Incapacitated condition, or a willing one, and ask.
For the duration, you can ask the target up to five questions, one per minute. For each question, the target makes a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, the memory the question reaches comes up whether the target wants it to or not, and you see it as the target saw it.
You see what it remembers, not what is true. A memory that was wrong when it was made comes back wrong, and a creature that has been lied to hands you the lie in perfect good faith.
The target knows afterwards what it was asked, and knows which of those questions it answered.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You can ask two additional questions for each spell slot level above 2.

school: Transmutation

components: V, S, M (a cap of dried mushroom)
duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Choose a point on the ground within range. A 15-foot-radius patch of pale fungus grows there over one round and remains for the duration. The area is Difficult Terrain.

Eyes open across the skin of the creature you touch: on the back of the head, the shoulders, the backs of the hands. They are its own, and it will be a while before it stops flinching at what they send it.
For the duration, the creature cannot be surprised while conscious, it cannot have the Blinded condition imposed by anything that covers only part of its head, and attack rolls against it do not gain Advantage for being made from behind or from an unseen angle.
It also has Disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks made against anyone who can see it, for reasons nobody has ever had to explain.

== Know the Enemy
level: 2
school: Divination
classes: Bard, Cleric, Ranger, Wizard
casting: 1 minute
range: Self
components: V, S
duration: Instantaneous
body:
Memory is not only what you lived. It is everything that ever went in, including what you read once and forgot you had read.
Name one kind of creature, one organisation or one place. You bring up everything you have ever learned about it, whether or not you remember learning it, and the GM tells you one true and useful fact: a resistance, a habit, a weakness, a name, a way in.
What comes up is what went in. If everything you were ever told about it was wrong, this spell hands you that, in perfect good faith and in detail.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You learn one additional fact for each spell slot level above 2.
== Soothe the Fever
level: 1
school: Abjuration
classes: Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger
casting: Action
range: Touch
components: V, S
duration: 24 hours
body:
The pulse itself, steadied. It is the first thing anyone in this Principle learns and the last thing they stop being asked for.
The creature you touch is no longer Poisoned, and for the duration it has Advantage on saving throws made against disease and poison, and against the effects of extreme heat or cold.
It does not cure a disease the creature already has. What it does is give the body back the days it needed, which for most people in most places is the whole of medicine.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You can touch one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.

== Shrug Off
level: 2
school: Abjuration
classes: Bard, Cleric, Druid, Paladin
casting: Bonus Action
range: Self
components: V
duration: Instantaneous
body:
Not a cure and not a defence. A body can be made to put one thing aside for a while and pay for it afterwards, and this is that.
Choose one condition currently on you from the following: Blinded, Deafened, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned or Stunned. It is suppressed for 1 minute, or until you finish a Short Rest, whichever comes first.
The condition is not gone and its duration goes on running underneath. When the suppression ends, whatever is left of it comes back, and you have the Exhausted condition with 1 Exhaustion level.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You can suppress one additional condition for each spell slot level above 2.

== Fever
level: 3
school: Necromancy
classes: Cleric, Druid, Warlock, Wizard
casting: Action
range: 60 feet
components: V, S
duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
body:
The pulse driven past what it can carry. It is the same operation as steadying somebody, run the wrong way, and every school teaches them together for that reason.
One creature you can see within range makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, for the duration it burns from the inside: it takes 4d6 Fire damage immediately and 2d6 Fire damage at the start of each of its turns, and it has Disadvantage on Constitution saving throws.
The creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. A creature that is immune to disease has Advantage on every one of these saves.

== Unshakable Chill
level: 1
school: Necromancy
classes: Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
casting: Action
range: 60 feet
components: V, S
duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
body:
The cold that the dead are cold with, put into somebody who is not one yet. It does not spread and it does not kill; it just will not go.
One creature you can see within range makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, for the duration it takes 1d6 Cold damage at the start of each of its turns, it loses any Resistance or Immunity it has to Cold damage, and it cannot benefit from a Short Rest.
No fire warms it while this lasts, and it knows that, which is most of what this spell is for.

== Wracking
level: 4
school: Necromancy
classes: Cleric, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
casting: Action
range: 30 feet
components: V, S
duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
body:
One creature you can see within range makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, its body is pulled out of true for the duration: limbs lengthen, joints turn the wrong way, and everything it does costs it more than it should.
While affected, the creature takes 4d8 Necrotic damage immediately, its Speed is halved, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and on Strength and Dexterity checks, and it cannot use any item that has to be held in two hands.
The creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. Magical healing of any kind also ends it.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 4.

== The Slow Stop
level: 6
school: Necromancy
classes: Cleric, Warlock, Wizard
casting: Action
range: 60 feet
components: V, S, M (a stopped watch, which the spell consumes)
duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
body:
Everything that lives is on its way to stopping. This does not cause that; it only takes the long way it was going and makes it short.
One creature you can see within range makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, for the duration it takes 6d8 Necrotic damage immediately and 3d8 Necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns, and its Hit Point maximum is reduced by the total Necrotic damage it has taken from this spell.
The creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
The reduction to its Hit Point maximum lasts until it finishes a Long Rest. A creature killed by this spell dies of ordinary causes, at the ordinary speed, all at once, and cannot be told apart afterwards from one that simply stopped.

== Atavism
level: 3
school: Transmutation
classes: Druid, Ranger
casting: Action
range: Touch
components: V, S, M (a tooth from something extinct)
duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
body:
Every animal is a shortened version of something that used to be larger and less reasonable. The Pact gives you leave to ask for that back, briefly.

== Thicket
level: 2
school: Transmutation
classes: Druid, Ranger
casting: 1 minute
range: 30 feet
components: V, S
duration: 8 hours
body:
Sprouting governs how a bonded plant grows, and the oldest use of it is not a weapon. It is a roof.
Branches, brambles and roots weave themselves into a shelter big enough for eight people, in a space you choose within range where plants are growing. It keeps out rain, wind and ordinary cold, it is Heavily Obscured from outside, and it has one entrance that closes over.
A creature inside gains the benefit of a Short or Long Rest as though sheltered and dry. The thicket has AC 12 and 40 Hit Points, and it is flammable.
When the spell ends, the plants go back to growing the way they were, which after eight hours of this is not quite the way they were before.

== Bough Hammer
level: 3
school: Transmutation
classes: Druid, Ranger
casting: Bonus Action
range: 60 feet
components: V, S
duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
body:
A bonded tree is asked to swing one of its own limbs. It is not made into anything and nothing is made out of it: what the spell governs is the growing, and a branch that grows fast enough in one direction is a hammer.
Choose a tree or a large plant you can see within range. When you cast this spell, and as a Bonus Action on each of your later turns, one of its boughs swings at a creature within 15 feet of it. Make a melee spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 3d8 Bludgeoning damage and must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be knocked Prone.
The tree does not move, and the spell ends if the tree is destroyed or if you move more than 60 feet from it.
Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 3.
