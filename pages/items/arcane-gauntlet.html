Arcane Gauntlet

Arcane focus · Lex Arcanum

Description

A finely crafted steel gauntlet etched with subtle runes of power, designed to channel magical energy directly through the wielder's hand.

Unlike traditional arcane foci such as wands, staves, or orbs, the Arcane Gauntlet is worn rather than held. This allows spellcasters—especially martial arcanists—to focus their magic without occupying a hand that could otherwise hold a weapon, shield, or perform physical tasks.

Special Ability

Hands-Free Focus — This gauntlet counts as an Arcane Focus. While wearing it on a hand, that same hand counts as a free hand for performing the Somatic (S) and Material (M) components of your spells, provided the hand is not actively holding another item or shield.

Who Uses It

  • Magister — Key part of your starting equipment and primary choice for your Spellcasting feature, seamlessly integrating with your martial combat styles.
  • Wizards, Sorcerers & Warlocks — Any arcane caster seeking to keep a hand open for grappling, climbing, or wielding a light weapon without juggling items during combat.

Notes

The Arcane Gauntlet is non-magical equipment and does not grant bonus attack rolls, bonus spell save DCs, or extra damage on its own. It requires no attunement.

It follows the rules for Arcane Foci: it can substitute for spell components that do not list a specific gold cost or are not consumed by the spell.

Interaction with War Caster: The War Caster feat already allows you to perform the Somatic (S) components of spells even while holding weapons or a Shield in one or both hands. If you take the War Caster feat, its Somatic benefit completely overlaps with the gauntlet's ability. However, the Arcane Gauntlet remains essential as a physical Arcane Focus, satisfying the Material (M) components of your spells without needing to hold a wand, staff, or orb.
