Arcane Gauntlet

Arcane focus · Lex Arcanum

Description

Plated in engraved steel and jointed so the fingers stay loose, this gauntlet can be used as a spellcasting focus for your spells.

Every Magister is issued one on admission to the order, and every Magister learns to fence around it. A duelist of the Lex Arcanum keeps a blade in the right hand and the gauntlet in the left, and treats the second as a weapon in its own right.

Special ability

Signum — The hand wearing this gauntlet counts as a free hand for performing the Somatic components of your spells, as long as that hand is not holding a weapon or a Shield.

Who uses it

  • Magister — part of your starting equipment, and the focus named by your Spellcasting feature. Your Arcane Swordplay requires the hand that wears it to stay free.
  • Gematurge — sets a cut draconic gem into the gauntlet, which recolors the magic that passes through it. The Jeweled Gauntlet is this item, modified.
  • Any other spellcaster may wear one, though few bother: it does nothing an orb or a wand does not, unless you need the hand back.

Notes

The gauntlet exists because of a single line in the spellcasting rules: a Somatic component requires at least one free hand. A held focus — an orb, a rod, a spellbook — occupies that hand, which is why most casters who fight with a weapon cannot also cast freely. A worn focus does not, and that is the whole trick. The same hand also reaches the Material components a focus supplies, so one free hand covers both.

Signum only makes a hand count as free. If some other effect already lets you perform Somatic components with weapons or a Shield in one or both hands, it does everything Signum does and more, and Signum adds nothing on top of it.

The gauntlet is ordinary equipment, not a magic item. It grants no bonus to attack rolls, damage or save DCs, and needs no attunement. A Magister who loses one mid-fight can still cast: the bare hand satisfies the Somatic component on its own, and only spells with Material components go dark until a focus is back.
