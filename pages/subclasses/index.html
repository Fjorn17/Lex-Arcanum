The three paths of the Magister

Only Gematurge has been written up so far. The other two are concepts and will most likely be reworked or dropped.

At Magister level 3 you choose a subclass, gaining further features at levels 5 and 9.

Gematurge

The jeweller — draconic gems cut for the gauntlet

In development

Cut crystalline vessels, set one into the Arcane Gauntlet and every spell you cast through it changes element. Push the discipline far enough and your own body starts to answer: crystal scales, draconic wings, a breath of pure energy.

Read the full subclass →

Weaver

The puppeteer — the Weave as thread, paid for in blood

Likely to be cut

Spin filaments of solidified magic out of your own blood and intercept the threads that tie mind to flesh, binding the living and hanging the dead on strings.

Sigilist

The engraver — spells fixed onto steel

Likely to be cut

Transcribe a spell directly onto a weapon with calligrapher’s supplies and costly ink, turning the blade into arcane storage that anyone holding it can release.

Homebrew for Baldur’s Gate 3 and D&D 5e. Nothing here is final.
