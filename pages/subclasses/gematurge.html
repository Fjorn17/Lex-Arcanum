Gematurge

Magister subclass · Lex Arcanum

Work in progress

This is the first subclass being written up, and it is still a draft: numbers and wording are being tested and will change. The features at Magister levels 13 and 17 are written, but provisional.

The doctrine of the gematurges is founded on emulating the singular nature of the gem dragons. These Magisters cut and refine crystalline vessels able to hold devastating elemental forces. Mastery of the discipline allows the partial transmutation of the gematurge’s own body, manifesting properties of those creatures such as scales of crystal or the ability to project bursts of pure energy.

A Gematurge gains features at Magister levels 3, 5, 9, 13 and 17, as shown in the Magister Class Progression table.

Level 3 Jewelled Gauntlet

You have learned to cut draconic gems capable of channelling elemental arcane energy through your Jewelled Gauntlet.

To cut one of these gems you must use your jeweller’s tools and work on a base gem worth at least 100 gp. The process requires 8 hours of work, which you can carry out as part of a Long Rest.

When you finish a Short or Long Rest, you can set one of your cut gems into your Jewelled Gauntlet, replacing any gem previously set. You can only have one gem set in your Jewelled Gauntlet at a time.

Level 3 Gem Resonance

The arcane energy you channel takes on the properties of the gem set in your Jewelled Gauntlet. damage dealt by your Magister spells and by your Magister features becomes the damage type associated with your gem.

Level 3 Draconic Jewellery

You have been instructed in the elitist techniques of gemmology and the secrets of the power latent within gems. You gain the following benefits:

Tool proficiency. You gain proficiency with jeweller’s tools. If you were already proficient with them, you gain proficiency with another type of artisan’s tools of your choice.

Gem cutting. When you craft an arcane focus, the total time required to create it is halved.

Level 5 Crystal Breath

While you are under the effects of Dragon's Breath, you can use its exhalation as part of your Attack action, replacing one of your attacks.

In addition, the spell takes on further properties according to the gem set in your Jewelled Gauntlet:

Level 9 Dragon Wings

While you are under the effects of Fly, draconic wings imbued with your gem’s element sprout from your back.

Once per turn, if you fly at least 6 metres straight down towards a creature immediately before hitting it with a melee attack roll, the attack deals an extra 2d6 damage of the type associated with the gem set in your Jewelled Gauntlet.

Creatures within 3 metres of the target must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Magister spell save DC. On a failed save they take 2d6 damage of the type associated with the gem set in your Jewelled Gauntlet; on a successful one, half as much.

With a higher-level spell slot. The attack’s extra damage and the area damage increase by 1d6 for each slot level above 3 used to cast Fly.

Level 13 Scaled Skin

When you cast Stoneskin on yourself, your gem’s energy fuses with the spell and crystalline scales sprout across your body.

While you maintain your concentration on the spell, you gain resistance to the damage type associated with the gem set in your Jewelled Gauntlet, and you have advantage on saving throws made to maintain that concentration.

Level 17 Gem Dragon Spirit

When you cast Summon Dragon, the spell summons a Gem Draconic Spirit instead of an ordinary Draconic Spirit. The Gem Draconic Spirit uses the properties associated with the gem set in your Jewelled Gauntlet at the moment you cast the spell.

Traits

Draconic Mount. The spirit is trained to serve you as a mount. When it appears, it also manifests an arcane saddle and harness fitted to you.

Gravity Field (Amethyst). Hostile creatures treat the area within 3 metres of the spirit as Difficult Terrain.

Refracted Radiance (Crystal). The spirit sheds Bright Light in a 6-metre radius and Dim Light for an additional 6 metres.

Tunneller (Emerald). The spirit can burrow through solid rock at half its Speed, leaving a tunnel 4.5 metres in diameter behind it.

Spider Climb (Sapphire). The spirit can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without making an ability check.

Amphibious (Topaz). The spirit can breathe air and water, and gains a Swim Speed of 9 metres.

Actions

Multiattack. The spirit makes a number of Rend attacks equal to half the spell’s level (rounded down) and uses its Breath Weapon.

Rend. Melee Attack Roll: bonus equals your spell attack modifier, reach 3 m. Hit: 1d6 plus 4 plus the spell’s level Slashing damage, plus 1d6 damage of the type associated with the gem.

Breath Weapon. The spirit exhales draconic energy in a 9-metre Cone.
