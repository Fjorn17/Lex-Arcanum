Introduction to Schematics

The creation of permanent magical equipment requires absolute architectural understanding of the weave, guided by ancient patterns etched in stone and metal.

In the arcane traditions of the order, these master plans are known as Schematics (or Arcane Formulas). A Schematic is a meticulously inscribed metallic plate, vellum scroll, or crystal matrix detailing the precise geometric layout, runic resonance nodes, and harmonic frequencies required to anchor a spell permanently into steel or leather.

Without a verified Schematic of the appropriate rarity, attempting to craft a permanent magic item results in catastrophic arcane feedback and the absolute ruin of the base materials.

Core Requirements for Crafting

To forge a permanent magic item using a Schematic, an artisan must meet the following criteria:

  • Tool Proficiency. You must have proficiency with the artisan's tools associated with the item (such as smith's tools for weapons or heavy armor, and jeweler's tools for rings or embedded foci).
  • Arcana Proficiency. You must possess proficiency in the Arcana skill.
  • The Schematic. You must possess the physical Schematic corresponding to the specific enchantment or item you wish to forge.
  • Base Item. You must supply or craft the nonmagical base object (such as a standard sword or suit of plate armor) to receive the enchantment.

Crafting Tiers and Costs

The time, financial investment, and minimum caster level required to bring a Schematic to life depend entirely on the item's rarity, as outlined below:

Rarity Minimum Level Creation Cost Time Required
Common 3rd 100 gp 4 days
Uncommon 3rd 500 gp 20 days
Rare 6th 5,000 gp 200 days
Very Rare 11th 50,000 gp 2,000 days
Legendary 17th 500,000 gp 20,000 days

Work Pace. Progress is made by investing increments of labor per day. These days do not need to be consecutive; an adventurer can pause their crafting to undertake quests, returning to their forge and continuing their work whenever downtime permits.
