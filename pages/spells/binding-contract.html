Lex Arcanum Magister Subclasses Spells

Binding Contract

Level 4 · Abjuration · Lex Arcanum

Description

Two willing parties swear an agreement sealed in gold. A spell sleeps inside it, and falls on whoever breaks it.

As you cast this spell, two willing creatures clasp hands and recite aloud the terms of an agreement between them. Each must understand the language spoken and accept the terms freely; the spell fails if either creature is Charmed or otherwise magically coerced. When the casting ends, a golden rune flares briefly in both signatories’ palms.

As part of casting this spell, you also cast a spell you have prepared, which is stored in the contract instead of taking effect. Casting it this way doesn’t expend a spell slot of its own, but you must provide its components as normal, and any material component it consumes is consumed as the contract is sealed. The stored spell must be of a level no higher than the slot you used for Binding Contract, must have a casting time of an Action or a Bonus Action, and must target a single creature or an area.

If a signatory knowingly breaks a term of the contract, two things happen at once.

Notice. Every other signatory instantly knows that the contract has been broken and by whom, regardless of distance or plane of existence.

Enforcement. The stored spell takes effect on the offender, centered on it if the spell affects an area. The offender signed the terms, so the spell can’t miss it: an attack roll against the offender hits automatically, and the offender automatically fails any saving throw or ability check the stored spell calls for. Any other creature caught in the spell resolves it normally, using your spell attack modifier or spell save DC as of the moment you cast this spell. The contract then ends.

The contract also ends if every signatory agrees to dissolve it, or if it is targeted by Dispel Magic cast using a spell slot of level 4 or higher. The DM determines whether a given act breaks a term.

At higher levels

With a higher-level spell slot. One additional willing creature can join the contract for each slot level above 4. Every signatory must be present, join hands in the circle and recite the terms. A higher-level slot also raises the level of the spell the contract can store.

How to learn

  • Magister — available from level 13, with the first 4th-level slots.
  • Wizard — available from level 7.
