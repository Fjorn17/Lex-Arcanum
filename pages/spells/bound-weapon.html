Bound Weapon

Level 1 · Conjuration · Homebrew

Description

You forge an magic bond with a weapon, ensuring it always returns to your hand when needed most.

You must wield the weapon throughout the casting, at the end of which you forge the bond. The bond fails if another creature is already bound to the weapon or if the weapon is a magic item attuned to someone else.

Once bound, you cannot be disarmed of the weapon unless you have the Incapacitated condition. If the weapon is on the same plane of existence, you can summon it as a Bonus Action, instantly teleporting it into your empty hand.

You can have a maximum number of bound weapons at one time equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of 1). If you attempt to bind a new weapon while at your limit, the weapon with the oldest bond immediately loses its bond.

Extended Ritual. If you cast this spell as a ritual, you can choose to extend its casting time to 1 hour instead of the usual 10 minutes. If you do so, the duration of the bond becomes permanent until targeted by Dispel Magic or brought into an Antimagic Field.

How to learn

  • Magister — available from level 1.
