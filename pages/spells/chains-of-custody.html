Chains of Custody

Level 3 · Conjuration · Lex Arcanum

Description

Spectral chains bind a creature where it stands. It can still move and fight, but it cannot leave — and every spell it casts costs it blood.

Spectral chains erupt from the ground and shackle a creature you can see within range. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the chains bind the target where it stands for the duration, and if it is flying, it falls, taking damage from the fall as normal.

While bound, the target can move normally, but it can’t willingly move more than 4.5 m (15 ft) from the space where the chains took hold. Whenever it casts a spell, it takes 1d8 Force damage, plus 1d8 Force for each level of the spell slot it expended.

The target, or a creature within 1.5 m (5 ft) of it, can take the Action to make a Strength (Athletics) check against your spell save DC, ending the spell on a success.

At higher levels

With a higher-level spell slot. Your Concentration can last longer with a level 4 spell slot (up to 1 hour) or a level 5 or higher slot (up to 8 hours).

How to learn

  • Magister — available from level 9, with the first 3rd-level slots.
  • Wizard — available from level 5.
