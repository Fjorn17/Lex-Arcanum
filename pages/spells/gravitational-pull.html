Gravitational Pull

Cantrip · Transmutation · Lex Arcanum

Description

A brief crease in gravity drags everything in a short cone one step toward you — loose objects included.

You extend your hand and fold gravity for an instant. Each Large or smaller creature in a 4.5 m (15 ft) cone must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 1d4 Force damage and is pulled 1.5 m (5 ft) in a straight line toward you.

Loose objects in the cone that weigh 4.5 kg (10 lb) or less and aren’t being worn or carried are pulled to your feet. If you have a free hand, you can catch one of them as part of the same action.

Cantrip upgrade

The damage increases by 1d4 Force when you reach Magister levels 5 (2d4 Force), 11 (3d4 Force) and 17 (4d4 Force).

How to learn

  • Wizard — available from level 1.
  • Magister — only as a Gematurge, from level 3, while an Amethyst gem is set in your Jeweled Gauntlet. It is not on the Magister spell list.
