Extradimensional Armoury

Level 1 · Abjuration · Lex Arcanum

Description

Stores a bound weapon in a pocket dimension the caster can open on a Bonus Action to draw the weapon or put it away.

You touch a weapon and bind it to a pocket dimension connected to you. For the duration of the spell, the weapon remains stored in that extradimensional space.

As a Bonus Action, you can open a circular portal of up to 0.5 metres in radius in an unoccupied space within 1.5 metres of you. You can make the stored weapon appear in one of your hands, or return it immediately to the pocket dimension through the portal.

The portal stays open until the end of the action and disappears immediately afterwards.

If a weapon is still stored in the pocket dimension when the spell ends, it appears in the nearest unoccupied space to you.

If you cast this spell again, the previous casting’s effect ends immediately.

At higher levels

With a higher-level spell slot. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of level 2 or higher, you can bind and store one additional weapon for each slot level above 1.

How to learn

  • Magister — available from level 1, with the first 1st-level slots.
