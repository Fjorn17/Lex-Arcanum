Arcane Thrust

Cantrip · Evocation · Lex Arcanum

Description

A thrust that runs force down the blade and past its point: the point reaches farther and takes the footing from whoever fails to hold it.

You lunge, and a thread of arcane force runs down the blade and out past its point. As you cast this spell, you make one melee attack with the weapon used in the casting.

For that attack the weapon gains the Reach property, adding 1.5 m (5 ft) to your reach with it.

On a hit, the target takes the weapon’s normal damage and must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be left off balance until the end of its next turn. While off balance, a creature has Disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity checks, and attack rolls against it have Advantage.

At higher levels

Cantrip upgrade. The attack deals an extra 1d4 Force damage when you reach level 5, 2d4 Force at level 11 and 3d4 Force at level 17.

How to learn

  • Magister — available from level 1, with your first cantrips.
